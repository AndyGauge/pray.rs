These are the prayers people chose to make **public** on [pray.rs](https://pray.rs):
what they asked for, how it was answered, and how many have prayed along.

They're fetched from the app over [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) each time this
site is built, without any author names, and they aren't kept anywhere else: when
someone makes a prayer private again, or releases it, it leaves this page at the next
build.
